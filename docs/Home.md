# Memory Prism — Documentation

> A structural framework for understanding persistence under constraint.

Memory Prism is a way to enter unfamiliar topics by asking what conditions make a phenomenon possible, what moves or accumulates, what persists, and where change or failure occurs. The goal is to find a useful route into an investigation—not to force every topic into the same shape.

## The basic movement

```text
Observation or puzzle
        ↓
Useful constraints and conditions
        ↓
Movement, accumulation, persistence
        ↓
Change, correction, failure, or uncertainty
        ↓
A better next question
```

This is a loose route, not a mandatory sequence. Real investigations loop, skip steps, and branch into domain-specific research.

## The source boundary

The Starter Kit is derived from the Memory Prism Framework and exactly these seven essays:

1. Boards
2. Audits
3. Identity
4. Flow Under Constraint
5. Working Lines
6. No Sacred States
7. Accumulation Patterns

The essays are not a seven-stage procedure. A lens earns its place only when it changes what becomes visible or what should be investigated next.

## Principles for applying it

- Answer settled questions directly.
- Keep evidence separate from inference and speculation.
- Prefer domain mechanisms over a neat analogy.
- Do the next obvious piece of research rather than making the user direct every step.
- Do not infer intention, sensing, correction, or governance where none is established.
- Stop when there is a productive next question.
