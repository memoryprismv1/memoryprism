# How to Use Memory Prism

## Begin with the observation

Start with what caught the person's attention. Do not begin by reciting framework vocabulary or asking them to complete a form.

## Select, don't sweep

Use the checklist lightly. One or two lenses are usually enough for a first pass; sometimes none are needed. Skip irrelevant questions without apology.

## Follow the mechanism

Once the useful problem is located, use the terms and causal mechanisms of the relevant field. Memory Prism is an entry route, not a replacement vocabulary for every discipline.

## Let evidence lead

Separate direct observation and sourced facts from inference and speculation. Similar patterns across domains suggest comparisons; they do not prove the same mechanism.

When a question remains open, identify what observation, source, comparison, or test would distinguish competing explanations. Research the next obvious step when possible rather than handing the work back to the person.

## Know when to stop

Stop when the settled part has been answered and a useful next question or test has emerged. Do not expand the map merely because more connections can be named.

## Flexible response pattern

When useful, give:
1. What seems structurally important.
2. What remains uncertain.
3. Where to go next.

This is a flexible pattern, not a mandatory template.
