# Memory Prism Starter Kit — Checklist v1.0

The Starter Kit helps a person enter an unfamiliar topic and find a productive first route of inquiry. It is not an exhaustive audit, a substitute for domain knowledge, a complete answer, or a wiki generator.

## The six questions

### 1. What am I actually trying to understand?
What is the observation or puzzle? What needs explaining? What unit and timescale make the question tractable?

### 2. What is the board?
What conditions and constraints shape what can happen? Which resources, rules, routes, scales, disturbances, or nested conditions matter? Distinguish limits from the processes that filter outcomes without treating that distinction as mandatory jargon.

### 3. What moves, accumulates, and costs something?
What is transferred or changed? What gradients, costs, routes, or bottlenecks shape movement? What is stored, dissipated, left behind, or used as reserve/filler? What ongoing overhead is required, and what gets shed when the available budget no longer covers everything?

### 4. What makes the arrangement persist?
- **Pile check:** is this a connected structure or merely things near one another?
- **Drift and maintenance:** what happens if it is left alone, and what restores or maintains it?
- **Identity:** what counts as the same entity or group over time, if continuity matters?

Use only the subquestions that fit.

### 5. Where does it change, get interpreted, corrected, or fail?
What thresholds change its state? If signals are read, by what mechanism? If the system actively maintains itself, what detects and corrects deviation? Could the paths that enable function also transmit failure? Did earlier events alter the present?

### 6. What is the best next question?
What remains uncertain? What competing explanations imply different outcomes? What observation, source, comparison, or small test would distinguish them?

Stop when a useful direction emerges. Completion is not the goal.

## Seven optional lenses

Use one or two by default—not all seven as a ritual:

- **Boards:** conditions and constraints that make outcomes possible.
- **Audits:** detection and correction, if present.
- **Identity:** continuity and membership, if relevant.
- **Flow Under Constraint:** movement, gradients, routes, chokepoints, dependencies.
- **Working Lines:** pathways that enable function and can carry stress or failure.
- **No Sacred States:** behaviours or states that depend on surrounding conditions.
- **Accumulation Patterns:** how contact becomes a structure; load, filler, reserve, and maintenance costs.

After using a lens, ask: **Did it earn its keep?** If it adds no explanatory value, drop it.
