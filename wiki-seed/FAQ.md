# FAQ

## Is Memory Prism an exhaustive analysis protocol?
No. The Starter Kit is an entry point into inquiry. It helps find an initial route and stops when a productive next question appears.

## Must every question be answered?
No. Questions are prompts, not mandatory stages.

## Must every topic use all seven lenses?
No. The lenses are optional. Use only the ones that materially improve the explanation or next step.

## Is a board just physical geometry?
Not necessarily. Conditions may include physical constraints, resources, learned conventions, institutional rules, and nested local conditions. Make the actual mechanisms clear rather than leaning on vague metaphor.

## Does every system audit or correct itself?
No. Some systems simply change state when physical thresholds are crossed. Discuss detection, interpretation, correction, or governance only when the mechanism supports it.

## How are the pile check and identity different?
The pile check asks whether components form a connected structure or are merely nearby. Identity asks what counts as the same entity or group over time and how membership is maintained.

## Should an AI show its full analysis?
Usually not. Surface the useful observations, do the research needed, and follow the next question. The framework is there to improve inquiry, not make every answer sound alike.

## What happens when the checklist reaches an unresolved question?
Move into domain-specific research, comparison, or testing. Keep alternatives open until evidence distinguishes them.
