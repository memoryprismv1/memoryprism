# The Framework

The repository's foundational artifact is the [Memory Prism Framework PDF](https://github.com/memoryprismv1/memoryprism/blob/main/MemoryPrism-Framework.pdf).

The PDF defines the framework. The checklist and skill help apply it; they do not replace or silently redefine it.

## Source boundary for the Starter Kit

The Starter Kit is derived from the Framework and exactly these seven supporting essays:

- Boards
- Audits
- Identity
- Flow Under Constraint
- Working Lines
- No Sacred States
- Accumulation Patterns

Other essays may be applications or explorations, but they are not required sources for the Starter Kit.

## Interpretation rule

Examples demonstrate ideas; they do not automatically become definitions. A correspondence between two domains is a prompt to investigate, not proof that the same mechanism operates in both.

When the sources do not establish a claim, leave that uncertainty visible. Do not silently import assumptions and present them as Memory Prism doctrine.
